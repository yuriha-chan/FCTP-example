## End-to-end acceptance trace

`tests/circulation.test.ts` exercises an A mine of 2 ore, consumption of both ore to make 1 metal, transfer to port stock and loading 1 metal. Registry receives A's signed ExitEvent, then presents the exact evidence and manifest to B. B waits one tick to berth, unloads after two ticks, consumes imported A metal to make 1 refined metal after two ticks, reloads after one tick, and departs to produce a separately verified signed ExitEvent. B-native mining and manufacture are independently exercised with tick counts in the test. The Registry non-adoption window and a cargo-mismatch decision are also checked. See `docs/handoffs/circulation.md` and the test for detailed evidence.

The client exposes this path for observation. The latest `timeout 180s npm test` gate passed build, typechecking, ESLint, and all 81 runtime tests, including focused compiled-browser authoring and public-creations browsing. See `docs/handoffs/player-authoring-final-verification.md`. This evidence does not claim complete protocol/profile conformance or full manual UI coverage.
